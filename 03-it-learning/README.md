# 🎫 IT Service Desk & Ticketing Operations

This section documents my hands-on learning in IT service desk operations, focusing on incident management, service requests, ticket handling, prioritization, and Service Level Agreement (SLA) management.

It covers the fundamental processes used by IT support teams to record, track, troubleshoot, resolve, and escalate technical issues.

## 🛠️ Skills & Practical Experience

- **Ticket Management & Documentation:** Practised creating and managing support tickets by recording user information, issue descriptions, symptoms, troubleshooting steps, actions taken, and resolution details. Focused on maintaining clear documentation for effective communication and handovers.

- **Ticket Classification & Prioritization:** Learned how to categorize incidents and service requests based on their impact and urgency. Practised assigning priorities, directing tickets to the appropriate support teams, and escalating critical issues when necessary.

- **SLA Management:** Developed an understanding of Service Level Agreements (SLAs), including response and resolution targets. Learned how support teams monitor ticket deadlines, prioritize urgent issues, and ensure incidents are handled within agreed timeframes.

- **Hands-On Ticketing Platforms – Spiceworks & Zendesk:** Completed practical exercises using Spiceworks and Zendesk to explore help desk operations. Practised creating tickets, updating ticket statuses, assigning priorities, adding internal notes, responding to users, and monitoring ticket progress.
